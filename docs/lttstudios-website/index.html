How it works

From sheet row to published video — automated

1

Connect

OAuth into Facebook Pages, TikTok accounts, and Google Sheets. Granular permissions you can revoke any time.

2

Queue

Fill a Google Sheet row per video: URL, title, description, schedule time, target Page or TikTok account.

3

Publish

The bot picks up rows when the schedule time arrives, respecting rate limits, active hours, and per-Page warmup caps.

4

Analyze

The Monitor tab fetches insights 24 hours after publish so you can see what works without switching apps.
