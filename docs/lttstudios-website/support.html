Frequently Asked Questions

Common setup questions, OAuth issues, rate limits, and troubleshooting tips. If your question isn't here, email support@lttstudios.com.

Getting started

How do I install the bot on Windows?

1. Download the latest .zip from the download page.
2. Extract anywhere (Desktop, Documents, etc.).
3. Run setup_new_machine.bat once — it checks Python, creates a venv, installs dependencies, and validates configuration.
4. Run run_gui.bat to launch the app.

The setup script is idempotent — safe to re-run if anything fails.

What credentials do I need to provide?

You bring your own:

  • Meta Developer App (free) — for Facebook Page publishing
  • TikTok Developer App (free) — for TikTok publishing
  • Google Cloud OAuth credentials (free tier sufficient) — for Sheets + Drive access

The bot does not provide credentials. You enter them in the Settings tab on first run. We never see your credentials because there is no LTTStudios server.

How do I connect my Facebook Page?

Open the bot → click the Facebook Accounts tab → click Connect Facebook Account. Your browser opens the FB OAuth dialog. Log in, grant permissions, and you'll be redirected back to a confirmation page on lttstudios.com.

Back in the app, the list of Pages you admin will appear. Check the boxes for Pages you want to enable for publishing, then click Save.

Permissions & visibility

Why is my video showing as "public" but no one can see it?

This is the #1 question from new users. The cause is almost always: your Meta App is in Development Mode and has not passed App Review.

Facebook silently hides posts created via apps that haven't been reviewed, even though they appear "Published" and "Public" in Business Suite. To fix: go through Meta App Review for pages_manage_posts permission. See our about page for how we're working through this ourselves.

I see a red dot next to my post saying "Hạn chế hiển thị" ("Reduced distribution"). Why?

Facebook's spam filter has reduced organic reach for that post. Common triggers:

  • Too many hashtags (FB recommends ≤ 5)
  • External links promoting subscriptions ("youtube.com/...?sub_confirmation=1")
  • "Engagement bait" phrases ("Comment below!" "Tag a friend!")
  • New Page with low trust score
  • Content that triggers nudity / violence / misinformation classifiers (even false positives)

Fix: review the caption, remove triggers, and write more naturally. The bot itself does not add hashtags or CTAs unless you enable the Caption Rotator feature.

Rate limits & scheduling

How often can the bot publish to one Page?

Defaults are conservative:

  • min_interval_per_page_s = 14400 — 4 hours between posts on the same Page
  • min_inter_page_interval_s = 900 — 15 minutes between posts on different Pages
  • max_posts_per_page_per_day = 3 — hard cap per Page per day
  • jitter_s = 600 — adds ±10 minute random delay

These are tuned to stay well within Facebook's spam thresholds. You can adjust them in Settings, but doing so increases the risk of Page warnings.

What time format should I use in the schedule_time column?

The bot accepts 8 formats:

2026-06-15 14:30        ← recommended
2026-06-15 14:30:00
15/06/2026 14:30
15-06-2026 14:30
2026-06-15T14:30
... and serial date floats from Google Sheets

Timezone follows the timezone config (default Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh = UTC+7).

To publish immediately, what time should I enter?

Enter the current time or up to 60 minutes in the past. The bot will pick up any row where schedule_time ≤ now and now - schedule_time ≤ skip_overdue_minutes (default 60).

Tip: use =NOW() in Google Sheets, then copy as value.

Common errors

Error: "(#100) Length of param title must be less than or equal to 255"

Facebook caps the video title at 255 characters. Shorten the title in your sheet's tieu_de_seo column.

If Caption Rotator is enabled, it may be wrapping your title with extra text that pushes it over the limit. Either disable Caption Rotator or use a shorter base title.

Error: "Token Page hết hạn" / Code 190

The Facebook Page access token has expired (long-lived tokens last ~60 days). Re-OAuth that account:

Settings → FB Accounts → click Reconnect next to the affected account. New token will replace the expired one. Past posts and audit logs are unaffected.

Anti-spam says "video tương đồng" (perceptual hash match) but it's a new video

The perceptual hash algorithm in older bot versions had a known weakness producing false-positive matches. Update to the latest version which tightens the matching threshold and rejects degenerate hash values.

If false positives persist, you can disable perceptual hash dedup temporarily by setting anti_spam_dedup = false (note: this also disables strict SHA dedup — re-enable after upload).

Video uploads as Reel even though I set post_type=feed. Why?

Facebook auto-classifies vertical (9:16) videos ≤ 90 seconds as Reels even when uploaded via the feed video endpoint. The bot logs will show facebook.com/reel/... instead of facebook.com/videos/....

To force feed (non-Reel) format: re-encode video to 16:9 (e.g., 1920×1080) OR ensure duration > 90 seconds. Both conditions need to be met for FB to keep it as a feed video.

OAuth callbacks & cross-machine

After approving OAuth in browser, the bot says "Bot không phản hồi"

The callback page (https://lttstudios.com/auth/fb-callback.html) tries to POST the OAuth code to the bot's local HTTPServer on 127.0.0.1:8765. Check:

  • Bot is still running when you complete OAuth
  • Windows Firewall isn't blocking port 8765 for the Python process
  • Try re-initiating OAuth from the bot (Settings → Reconnect)
Can I OAuth TikTok on a different machine (e.g., phone)?

Yes — the bot supports cross-machine TikTok OAuth via a QR code. Click Connect TikTok Account → a dialog shows a QR code containing the OAuth URL. Scan with your phone, complete TikTok login. The callback POSTs to a relay endpoint on lttstudios.com/api/oauth-relay/store.php, and the bot pulls the code from the relay.

This requires the relay PHP files to be uploaded to your hosting. If not configured, fall back to same-machine OAuth (open browser on the bot's PC).

Sheets & Drive

The bot can't see my sheet — "Permission denied"

Either:

  1. If using a Service Account: share the Sheet with the service account's email (visible in your service_account.json as client_email) with Editor permission.
  2. If using OAuth2: the OAuth flow opens browser on first run — log in with the Google account that owns / has access to the Sheet.
Video download from Google Drive fails with "virus scan warning"

For videos > 100MB Google shows a virus-scan confirmation page before download. The bot handles this automatically — it parses the confirmation form and submits it. If it still fails:

  • Verify the Drive link has "Anyone with link can view" permission
  • Check the bot has Drive read scope
  • Try the file in incognito browser to confirm it's accessible without login

Data & privacy

Where is my data stored?

Only on your machine. Tokens in data/tokens.enc and data/tiktok_tokens.enc (encrypted). Logs in logs/app.log. Audit trail in data/audit_log.jsonl. Cached videos in temp_videos/ (auto-deleted after upload).

Nothing is sent to LTTStudios servers — we don't have any.

How do I delete all data?

See the Data Deletion page for step-by-step instructions. Summary: close the bot, delete the installation folder, revoke the app at Facebook Settings → Business Integrations.

Still stuck?

Email support@lttstudios.com with: bot version, Windows version, what you tried, and the last 50 lines of logs/app.log. We reply within 5 business days.
