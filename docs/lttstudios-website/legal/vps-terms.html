LTT VPS Terms

Windows virtual servers · Effective: 2026-08-25

These terms cover the LTT VPS service specifically. The general LTT Studios Terms of Service still apply; where the two differ, this page wins, because it describes the service you are actually using.

Every number on this page is what the system actually enforces, not a target. An automated check keeps them in step with the software.

1. How the machine is delivered

You receive a blank machine with Windows installation media attached. You run the installer yourself through the browser console and set your own account and password.

  • We do not know your password and cannot reset it. What we never hold, we cannot leak.
  • Losing the password means reinstalling the machine, which means losing all data on it.
  • Everything inside the machine is yours to manage: patches, firewall, software, and who gets in.

2. Windows licensing — read this section

The price does NOT include a Windows licence. We supply installation media, not a licence.

  • The Windows Server 2016 and 2022 media are Microsoft Evaluation editions — 180-day trials.
  • Windows 10 LTSC requires its own volume licence.
  • When you run the installer, you accept Microsoft's licence agreement, and you are responsible for licensing your use correctly.

If you need licensed Windows for commercial use, obtain your own licence or use your own key. We do not resell Microsoft licences.

3. Billing and cycle

  • The cycle is 30 days, paid in advance with credit.
  • 1 credit = 100 VND.
  • Current price per cycle:
    • VPS Small — 1 vCPU · 2 GB RAM · 40 GB disk · 300 GB bandwidth — 149,000₫ (1,490 credit)
    • VPS Medium — 2 vCPU · 4 GB RAM · 60 GB disk · 600 GB bandwidth — 179,000₫ (1,790 credit)
    • VPS Large — 4 vCPU · 8 GB RAM · 80 GB disk · 1,000 GB bandwidth — 329,000₫ (3,290 credit)
    • Extra disk: per 10 GB — 25,000₫/cycle
  • At each renewal the system deducts credit automatically. Nothing for you to do.
  • A statement of every charge is on your machine's page.

4. Expiry, suspension, and deletion

This is the most important section here. We state it bluntly because the consequence cannot be undone.

  1. At renewal, if your credit balance is insufficient: the machine is suspended immediately — powered off, but your data is kept.
  2. You have 7 days from suspension to top up and resume.
  3. After 7 days the machine is permanently deleted — disk and all snapshots destroyed. We cannot restore it, even if you pay afterwards.

The dedicated IP address returns to the pool when a machine is deleted. A new machine will get a different address.

5. Resources and limits

  • RAM is dedicated. What you buy is reserved for you.
  • Disk size is a quota on shared storage, not a dedicated drive. You occupy what you actually use.
  • CPU is shared. Your machine can run slower while other machines on the same host are busy.
  • Bandwidth is capped per plan. Past the cap, speed is reduced to 1 MB/second for the rest of the cycle. We do not charge overage and we do not cut you off.

6. Snapshots — not backups

We snapshot your machine every 7 days and keep 1. You can roll back to the latest snapshot from your machine's page.

Understand what that gives you:

  • Snapshots live on the same storage as your machine. If that storage fails, both are lost. A snapshot does not replace your own backup.
  • Snapshots are taken while the machine runs — like pulling the power and booting again. They capture what has reached the disk, not everything as of that exact instant. Data written seconds earlier may not be in the snapshot.
  • Rolling back discards every change made after the snapshot, and cannot be undone.

Keep your own backup of anything that matters.

7. Acceptable use

The following are prohibited, and a violation leads to immediate suspension without notice:

  • Sending spam, or assisting the sending of spam in any form.
  • Port scanning, credential stuffing, denial-of-service attacks, or any attack directed at someone else's systems.
  • Storing or distributing content that breaks Vietnamese law or infringes someone else's intellectual property.
  • Cryptocurrency mining. CPU is shared, and one machine running flat out continuously slows every other customer's machine.
  • Reselling or resharing the service to third parties.

Outbound ports 25, 465 and 587 are blocked at the network layer for every machine. This is a technical control, not a policy you can request an exception to: one machine sending spam puts the whole system's addresses on blocklists, and then every customer pays for it. If your application needs to send mail, use a mail provider's API over HTTPS.

8. Availability

The service runs on a best-effort basis. We make no uptime commitment and offer no SLA.

  • Hosts may reboot for maintenance or updates.
  • Internet access reaches your machine through an intermediate server; if that server fails you temporarily cannot reach your machine, even though it is running.

If your work cannot tolerate interruption, this service is not yet a fit. We would rather say so now than have you find out at a bad moment.

9. Refunds

A cycle that has started is not refundable: the resources (RAM, disk, IP address) were reserved for you from the start of the cycle and cannot be resold to someone else.

You may delete a machine at any time; deletion stops future cycles and does not refund the current one.

If the service is unusable because of a fault on our side, contact support@lttstudios.com and we will look at it case by case.

10. Your data

  • We do not access the inside of your machine. We do not have your password.
  • The browser console shows your machine's screen. It opens only from your own account, using a single-use ticket that expires after 60 seconds.
  • Deleting a machine destroys the disk and every snapshot. It cannot be restored.
  • We retain machine lifecycle logs (created, started, stopped, suspended, deleted) and billing ledger entries, so that operational and accounting questions can be answered.

Details in the Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to these terms

We may revise this page. For changes that are adverse to you (price increases, reduced limits, new restrictions) we give 30 days' notice by email. If you disagree, you may delete your machine before the change takes effect.

12. Governing law

These terms are governed by the law of Vietnam. Disputes that cannot be settled by negotiation go to a competent court in Vietnam.
