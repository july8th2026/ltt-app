Cookie Policy

Short version: we don't use tracking cookies. Long version below.

Effective: 2026-06-02 · Version 1.0

What this policy covers

This Cookie Policy applies to the LTTStudios marketing website at lttstudios.com. It does NOT cover the desktop application (which doesn't use cookies — it's not a web app).

Cookies we use

None.

This website is fully static HTML/CSS/JavaScript. It does not set any cookies, does not use localStorage for tracking, does not have analytics scripts (Google Analytics, Plausible, Fathom, etc.), and does not embed any third-party widgets (chat boxes, social embeds, ads).

Why the short list?

We believe in privacy by design. The marketing site is information-only — it doesn't need to remember who you are between visits, so it doesn't try to.

If we ever need cookies (e.g., to remember language preference, or for a login flow if we add accounts), we will:

  1. Update this page with the cookie name, purpose, and expiration
  2. Show a clear consent banner before setting any non-essential cookie
  3. Default to opt-OUT for non-essential cookies (no dark patterns)

Third-party cookies on linked pages

When you click external links (e.g., to developers.facebook.com, github.com, etc.) those sites set their own cookies according to their own policies. We have no control over external sites.

How to disable cookies anyway

Even though we don't set any, you can disable cookies entirely in your browser. Search for "disable cookies in [your browser name]" for instructions. This site will continue to work normally.

Browser tracking protection

This site sends the Do Not Track signal through respect — not because we honor a specific DNT header, but because we have nothing to track in the first place.

Changes to this policy

If we ever start using cookies, this page will be updated and we will add a notice in the footer of the site for at least 30 days announcing the change.
