About

Built by creators, for creators

A small, focused Vietnam-based studio building the automation tools we wished existed when we ran our own brand pages.

Our story

LTTStudios was founded in 2024 by LE THANH TRUNG (LTT), a content creator who, after years of managing 20+ brand Facebook Pages and TikTok accounts by hand, finally got tired of clicking "Schedule post" 50 times a day.

The first version of LTTStudios VideoBot was a Python script that did one thing: read a Google Sheet row, download a video from Drive, post to a Facebook Page. The script grew. It learned about rate limits the hard way (Page warnings, then Page restrictions). It learned about content deduplication the hard way (videos flagged as spam). It learned about API quotas, OAuth refresh, and TikTok's content posting policies.

Today LTTStudios VideoBot is a polished desktop tool that handles everything we wished we'd had on day one: spam-policy-safe scheduling, multi-Page rate limit coordination, audit trails, engagement analytics, perceptual-hash deduplication, and a Vietnamese-first UI.

What we believe

Creators own their tools, their data, and their tokens

The bot runs on your machine. Your Facebook tokens never leave your computer. There is no LTTStudios backend that could be breached. There is no telemetry pinging "home" with your post stats. We do not have an admin panel that can read your data — because there is no panel.

Respect the platforms, respect the policies

Every feature in the bot is built to comply with Meta and TikTok policies. Rate limits are conservative by default (we'd rather under-post than risk a Page warning). Content dedup runs before every upload. Warmup logic prevents new Pages from being treated like established brands. Circuit breakers stop publishing when the platform tells us something is wrong.

Documentation is a feature

Every config key has an explanation. Every error has a Vietnamese message. The Help system has Quick Start guides for every tab. Source code is readable. This website you're reading is part of the product.

Where we are

LTTStudios is registered and operated from Mê Linh, Hà Nội, Vietnam. We do not have offices in other countries. We do not have venture funding. We do not have a sales team.

How we make money

Honest answer: LTTStudios VideoBot is currently free. The project is funded by LTT's own creator income. If we add paid tiers in the future, the free tier will remain functional for individual creators with a small number of Pages.

We do not run ads. We do not sell user data (we couldn't even if we wanted to — we don't have your data on our servers).

Our open commitments

  • No telemetry — the bot never reports back to any LTTStudios endpoint about your usage.
  • No upsells — no nag screens, no "premium" lock-outs.
  • No vendor lock-in — your Google Sheets are yours, your published videos are yours, your audit logs are yours.
  • Policy-aligned by default — all defaults err on the side of platform safety, even if that means slower posting.
  • Documented breakage — when Meta or TikTok change their APIs and we have to update the bot, we explain what changed and why in the changelog.

What's next

We're working on getting LTTStudios VideoBot fully approved by Meta App Review (in progress as of June 2026) and through the TikTok Direct Post audit (submitted May 2026). Once both are live, we plan to expand cross-posting between platforms while maintaining the same privacy-first desktop architecture.
